I like making people
feel something.

I'm Hrag, based in Yerevan, Armenia. Most of the time I'm either behind a camera, inside an edit, or explaining to someone why their shortcut should absolutely be on a keyboard.

I've stayed in EYP Media since 2022 because I love what happens when technical work and people collide. A good session video is not just a collection of nice shots. It's a way of bringing somebody back to a moment they thought they had already left behind.

I'm also very into experimentation. Traditional tools and methods got us here for a reason, but I don't see a reason to stop there. AI, new workflows and weird ideas can make things faster, stranger and sometimes simply better. The fun part is finding out which one it is.

And then there is the team. I like sharing the little tricks, workflows and random things I learn along the way. The goal is not just to finish the session. It's to have people leave knowing a little more than when they arrived.
